# Grant Sample 2: Private Family Foundation Program Proposal
**Project:** Family Forward | Familias Adelante (Bilingual In-Home Family Skill Building)  
**Funder:** The James B. and Lois R. Archer Charitable Foundation  
**Request Amount:** $50,000 Program Grant  
**Author:** Brandon Hinrichs  

---

## 1. Project Abstract
Family Forward | Familias Adelante is a voluntary, community-anchored prevention program designed to preserve families, prevent child welfare system involvement, and expand bilingual family coaching for Hispanic and Latino families across rural Nebraska counties. Anchored by a dedicated bilingual (English/Spanish) Family Skill Builder, the initiative provides individualized coaching in family communication, parenting stabilization, school advocacy, and healthcare navigation.

---

## 2. Statement of Community Need
Rural agricultural communities in central and southeastern Nebraska have seen substantial demographic growth over the past decade. Spanish-speaking families routinely encounter formidable language, cultural, and institutional access barriers when engaging local schools, healthcare providers, and social service agencies. 

Existing public family-support programs in the region operate almost exclusively through formal court mandates, child protective services (DHHS) referrals, or juvenile probation orders. Families in crisis cannot access skill-building support *before* an acute breakdown occurs. Family Forward eliminates this systemic deficit by offering 100% voluntary, non-stigmatizing, community-referred coaching with zero system involvement required.

---

## 3. Methodology & Program Architecture: "Do With — Not For"
Family Forward avoids one-size-fits-all classroom lectures, utilizing an evidence-informed "Do With — Not For" coaching model:
1. **Strengths-Based Intake:** The Family Skill Builder conducts an initial bilingual Family Strengths & Goals Assessment in the home or preferred community setting, establishing 30-, 60-, and 90-day family goals.
2. **Hands-On Skills Practice:** Rather than merely providing handouts, the Skill Builder accompanies parents to school conferences, assists in organizing medical paperwork, and models positive behavioral reinforcement techniques in real-world scenarios.
3. **Youth Empowerment & Relational Health:** Coaches youth in social-emotional skills, conflict de-escalation, and academic persistence while helping parents understand school tracking systems.
4. **Community Syndication:** Bridges families to local food security, legal clinics, health screenings, and bilingual library programs, creating lasting community safety nets.

---

## 4. Measurable Goals & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Enrollment Benchmark:** 20–25 rural families engaged in intensive 6-month coaching cycles annually.
* **Goal Attainment:** Over 80% of participating families achieve at least two primary self-identified stabilization goals on their 90-day review.
* **System Prevention:** 95%+ of enrolled families maintain stability with zero out-of-home foster care placements or formal court filings during program participation.
* **Linguistic Access:** 100% of intake assessments, resource directories, and coaching plans produced in bilingual English/Spanish formats.
