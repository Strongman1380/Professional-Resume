# Grant Sample 3: Foundation Letter of Inquiry (LOI)
**Project:** Housing Stability & Independent Living Support for Youth Aging Out of Care  
**Funder:** The DanPaul Foundation  
**Request Amount:** $15,000 Direct Support Grant  
**Author:** Brandon Hinrichs  

---

*(Executive Application Letter Format)*

**The Board of Directors**  
**The DanPaul Foundation**  

**RE: Grant Request — Transitional Housing & Independent Living for Youth Aging Out of Foster Care**

Dear Members of the Board:

On behalf of our organization, I am pleased to submit this request for a **$15,000 program grant** to expand transitional living services and direct housing support for young people aging out of Nebraska's foster care system.

### A Direct Mission Alignment
Your foundation's dedication to assisting youth in crisis and expanding opportunities for independent adulthood directly matches our frontline work. In the rural multi-county region we serve, young people aging out of the child welfare system at age 18 or 19 face an immediate structural cliff: zero dedicated transitional youth housing exists within an 8-county radius. Without family safety nets or rental history, these youth face extreme vulnerability to homelessness, exploitation, and uncompleted education.

### What the Grant Funds
A $15,000 grant provides catalytic, practical assistance that directly unlocks stable adulthood:
* **$7,500 in Rental & Security Deposit Subsidies:** Eliminates the insurmountable first/last-month deposit barrier, placing 5–7 youth into safe independent apartments.
* **$4,500 in Vocational & Academic Bridge Grants:** Covers trade-school tools, nursing scrub uniforms, community college textbook fees, and laptop hardware required to secure living-wage jobs.
* **$3,000 in Emergency Stabilization Funds:** Prevents catastrophic disruption by covering sudden utility bills, emergency prescription co-pays, and reliable transportation repairs.

### Proven Community Stewardship
Our programs combine safe physical housing with intensive one-on-one coaching: budgeting, credit establishment, lease comprehension, grocery planning, and mental health maintenance. In the past program year, **92% of youth in our transitional housing initiative maintained continuous housing stability or transitioned into permanent independent leases**, with 88% maintaining active employment or post-secondary enrollment.

Every dollar requested directly touches participant stability. We thank you for your dedication to vulnerable youth and welcome the opportunity to partner with the DanPaul Foundation.

Sincerely,

**Brandon Hinrichs**  
Grants & Development Coordinator
